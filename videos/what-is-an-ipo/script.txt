Aap ke dost ne group mein likha, 'Yaar, IPO aa raha hai, apply kiya?' Aap ne bhi 'haan' likh diya... lekin dil mein sawal tha. Ye IPO hai kia? Welcome to our 60 Seconds Investor series, jahan hum psx ke terms ko simple tareeqe se samjhate hain. Sochiye market mein ek naya phone launch hone wala hai. Company pehle pre-booking kholti hai, aur jo pehle book kare, usay launch price milti hai. IPO bhi bilkul aisa hi hai. IPO yani Initial Public Offering mein ek private company pehli baar apne shares aam logon ko bechti hai aur PSX par list hoti hai. ye process do stages mein hota hai. Pehle book building: jahan ek price band di jati hai, jaise 10 se 14 rupay, aur bade investors us mein bid lagate hain. ab Demand se jo final price nikalti hai, wo hoti hai strike price. Phir aati hai general public subscription jahan retail investors broker ke zariye usi strike price par apply karte hain. Lekin listing ke baad price fluctuate ho sakti hai, isliye investors ko chahiyay ke apni pouri research kar ke apply karein. Agar aap aise hi market ke terms aasaan zubaan mein samajhna chahtay hain to FloStocks ko follow karein.
