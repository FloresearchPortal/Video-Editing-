Sochiye ek bara pizza hai jo akele khareedna tou mushkil hai. Lekin us ka ek slice har koi le sakta hai.
REIT mein pizza ek bari property hoti hai, aur slice us ka ek chhota hissa.
REIT yani Real Estate Investment Trust mein bohat se investors ka paisa jama hota hai, jis se malls, offices ya apartments jaisi properties khareedi ya banai jati hain.
In properties se jo kiraya aur munafa aata hai, wo investors mein unke hisse ke hisab se taqseem hota hai.
REIT ke units aap PSX par share ki tarah khareed aur bech sakte hain, bina poori property khareede.
Lekin yaad rahay ke property market ke utar charhao se REIT ke units ki price aur kiraya dono badal sakte hain.
