Aap ne aajkal har taraf PSX ka naam tou suna hi hoga — 'Market upar, market neeche!' Lekin akhir ye sab hai kia? Mein samjhata houn!
Socheyay — aapke gali mein Ahmed ka ek chai ka dhaaba hai. Or chai itni zabardast hai ke rozana line lag jati hai. Ab Ahmed chahta hai ke wo shehar mein 5 dhaabe kholay — lekin uskepaas paisay bilkul nahi hain.
Ahmed decide karta hai apna dhaaba public ko thora thora bech de. Woh PSX par apni company list karta hai aur 1000 shares nikalta hai jahan har share ke qeemat houti hai 100 rupay. Is tarah Ahmed ko market se mil jaate hain 1 lakh rupay, bina kisi bank ke. Aur hum jaisay logon ko miltay hai Ahmed ke dhaabe mein hissay yani ke shares.
Aap sochte ho 'Yaar Ahmed ki chai toh kamal hai...' Toh aap 5 shares khareed lete ho 500 rupay mein. 6 mahine baad Ahmed ke 5 dhaabe chal rahe hotey hain. Or us share ki qeemat ho gayi 200 rupay. Aur Aapka 500 ghar bethay bethay ban jata hai 1000 rupay!
Lekin sawal ye hai ke share ki qeemat kyun badhi? Kyunke jab logon ko pata chala ke Ahmed ka business achi taraf ja raha hai — sab uskay shares khareednay lagay yani ke demand badh gayi. Lekin shares toh sirf market mein 1000 thay tou supply limited thei. Jab market mein demand zyada aur supply kam ho tou qeemat apne aap upar ajaati hai. Ab agar logo ko lagey Ahmed ka dhaaba dub raha hai? Sab bechne lagenge — supply zyada hojaie ge, demand kam hojaie ge, jis ke waja se qeemat bhe girna shouru hojaie ge.
Is market ke upper or neechay janay ko kehtay hain Market Volatility. Ye sirf numbers nahi hain. Ye laakhon logon ki soch — unka darr, unki umeed, unka bharosa hai. PSX asal mein aik regulated marketplace hai jahan Pakistani companies ke shares trade houtay hain.
Aur agar aap isi tarhan PSX ko aasaan zubaan mein samajhna chahte hain. Toh FloStocks ko follow karein. Kyunke hum stock market ko mushkil nahi, simple bana kar samjhate hain.
