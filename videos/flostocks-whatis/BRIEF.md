# "What is FloStocks?" — creative brief (keep every version consistent with this)

**Theme keywords (from the client): premium Apple-style UI × dynamic fintech motion graphics.**
- Apple side: white / off-white canvases, frosted-glass cards, soft layered shadows, big tight
  Inter (SF-like) headlines with grey secondary lines, generous spacing, smooth critically-damped
  springs (no bounce gimmicks), focus/blur depth, app-launch style zooms.
- Fintech side: live line charts, candlesticks, watchlists with green/red pills, number
  counters, tickers, order tickets, gauges — moving with energy, cut on the music.
- Brand: FloStocks by Floret Capitals (flostocks.com), green `#38d100` on white.

**Story**
1. iMessage chat (realistic, natural pace, thread from the top, no keyboard / input bar):
   i opened my account with floret capitals · PMEX? · Nope, PSX · Wo tou pmex ke top broker nai? ·
   yes they are · but · THEY ARE NOW IN STOCKS TOO!
2. The punchline bubble lifts (Apple focus), turns green and opens straight into the FloStocks
   section — **no full-screen green headline in between**.
3. Showreel: FloStocks = the official PSX division of Floret Capitals. PSX only — **no commodities
   / PMEX content** (the chat lines are the only PMEX mentions). Learning is guides, ebook and
   Flopedia — **never present the Bootcamp as a video series**.
4. End on "Open your PSX account" + flostocks.com + the market-risk disclaimer. Never promise returns.

**Motion rules**
- Fast-paced like the music: one bar per idea, entrances on beats/8ths/16ths; feature montages
  get a one-liner per element and a market graphic that moves on the beat. Music is instrumental only (no vocals).
- The punchline holds on screen, turns green exactly on a beat, then transitions to the logo.
- Learn section is a list (Apple grouped-list style), not a carousel. No SIP in the feature montage.
- Music: launch-video style track with beat drops; the motion is cut and keyed to its drops,
  stops and downbeats. Intro is silent (message sounds only); music fades in gently from "yes they are" and the beat drops at exactly 0:11.
- The CTA ending is fast (everything lands inside one bar) and short.
- No heartbeat / per-beat pulsing animations anywhere.
- Text never overlaps other text or crosses graphics.
- Deliver per-section previews for approval before compiling the final video.
